A make.fun entry for [The Robot Apology](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59).

A robot apologizes in two lines: one sentence owning a specific mistake, one sentence naming the lesson and the fix.

> I am sorry I sent your dentist appointment to all 214 people in your contacts, because I read "remind everyone" as a literal instruction.
> I have learned that "everyone" means the people in the room, not the people in the address book, so I will ask which everyone before I send anything again.

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) writes those two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
